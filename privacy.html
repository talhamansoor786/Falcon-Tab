🦅 Privacy Policy

Falcon New Tab  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Falcon New Tab is a Chrome browser extension that replaces the default new tab page with a customizable productivity dashboard. This policy explains what data the extension accesses, how it is used, and what we do (and do not) collect.

Data We Do NOT Collect

Falcon New Tab does not:

  • Collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.
  • Send your browsing history, bookmarks, or shortcuts to any server.
  • Use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party telemetry SDKs.
  • Create or maintain user accounts.
  • Share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Stored Locally in Your Browser

All settings and preferences are stored exclusively in Chrome's built-in storage APIs (chrome.storage.local and chrome.storage.sync) on your device. This includes:

  • Your configured RSS feed URLs and shortcut links
  • Dashboard layout (block order and sizes)
  • Temperature unit preference and theme choice
  • Quick notes content
  • Custom background image (stored locally only)
  • Cached weather and news data (temporary, stored locally)

chrome.storage.sync data is synced by Chrome across your own signed-in devices using Google's infrastructure — Falcon has no access to or visibility into this sync process.

Network Requests Made by the Extension

The extension makes outbound requests solely to deliver its features:

  • Weather Open-Meteo API
    Fetches current weather for your location from api.open-meteo.com. Your approximate GPS coordinates (or IP-derived coordinates) are sent to this API. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API that does not require an API key and does not track users. See their terms.
  • Location (fallback) BigDataCloud / FreeIPAPI
    If GPS is unavailable, an approximate location is derived from your IP address via api.bigdatacloud.net and freeipapi.com. No account or personal data is stored by these services beyond what is typical for any HTTP request.
  • RSS Feeds User-configured URLs
    Falcon fetches RSS feeds from URLs you explicitly configure in Settings. Requests are made from the background service worker directly to the feed source. No feed content passes through any Falcon-controlled server.
  • Icons & Fonts CDN
    Site shortcut icons may be fetched from Google's favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons) and Icons8 (img.icons8.com). Weather condition icons are loaded from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). These are standard CDN requests and do not carry any personally identifying payload beyond your IP address (standard for any web request).

Permissions Explained

  • storage — Save your settings and cached data locally.
  • bookmarks — Display and search your Chrome bookmarks in the dashboard.
  • topSites — Show your frequently visited sites in the Recent Sites widget.
  • notifications — Send a browser notification when your focus timer session ends.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — Required to fetch RSS feeds from any domain you choose to configure. Because users can add feeds from any website, the extension cannot predict and enumerate every possible feed URL in advance.

Children's Privacy

Falcon New Tab does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. No personal information is collected from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change. Significant changes will be noted in the extension's version release notes.
