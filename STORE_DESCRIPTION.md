## Full Description

Falcon New Tab transforms the default Chrome new tab page into a customizable productivity dashboard that keeps everything you need in one place.

**FEATURES**

**Quick Links & Bookmarks**
Access your favorite websites instantly with customizable shortcut tiles. Browse and search your entire Chrome bookmarks hierarchy through an interactive explorer, or add your own custom quick links.

**Recent Sites**
Return quickly to frequently visited websites using Chrome's Top Sites integration.

**Fully Customizable Dashboard**
Drag, drop, resize, and rearrange widgets to build a layout that fits your workflow. The dashboard automatically saves your arrangement and restores it every time you open a new tab.

**RSS News Reader**
Stay informed by adding any RSS feed from your favorite websites. Falcon merges stories from multiple sources into a single unified feed, sorts them by publication time (newest first), and displays article thumbnails where available.

**Stories Carousel**
Scan the latest headlines through a smooth horizontal scrolling experience designed for quick news discovery. Feeds can be added and managed from the Settings panel.

**Focus Timer**
Boost your productivity with a built-in focus timer featuring multiple presets, a visual progress ring, and a native browser notification when your session ends.

**Quick Notes**
Capture ideas, reminders, links, and tasks with an auto-saving notes widget. Content is stored locally and is always available the next time you open a new tab.

**Live Weather**
View current weather conditions and temperature for your location directly from your dashboard.

**Clock & Date**
A large, easy-to-read clock and date display is always visible at a glance.

**Custom Backgrounds**
Upload any image as your new tab background for a personalized, distraction-free experience.

**Light & Dark Themes**
Choose between beautifully crafted light and dark themes to match your environment or preference.

---

**PRIVACY FIRST**

Falcon New Tab stores all dashboard preferences locally in your browser. RSS feeds are fetched directly from sources you configure — no data passes through third-party servers, and no personal information is collected or sold.

---

Designed for users who want a faster, cleaner, and more productive new tab experience.
